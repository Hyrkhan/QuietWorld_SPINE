# Character Art Prompts — NPCs & UI Hero Previews

Same master-rules + swappable-SUBJECT format as the prop prompt. Paste the whole
**MASTER PROMPT** and replace `{{SUBJECT}}` with one line from the list at the bottom.

> 🎨 **Art direction:** finished **hand-painted chibi** like Pacifica's class
> portraits (style reference only, never copy their characters, logo or
> designs): clean inked lines, brushy cel shading, glossy hair, shiny leather.
> It should feel hand-painted, not AI-airbrushed. The subjects below are your
> own Verge cast.

> 🎨 **Palette canon source:** per-NPC colours below are pulled from
> [`ART_PROMPTS.md`](./ART_PROMPTS.md) and [`NPCS.md`](./NPCS.md). Those are the
> source of truth — every NPC has a **distinct non-bronze primary**. Treat the
> SUBJECT line as palette law; the master prompt's "default palette" is only a
> fallback for colours the SUBJECT doesn't name.

---

## MASTER PROMPT

```
Finished HAND-PAINTED DIGITAL CHIBI character art in the style of 2000s anime MMORPG class portraits (like Pacifica's bandit and archer class art): a cute, fully coloured, full-body chibi character, hand-inked and hand-painted in Paint Tool SAI / Photoshop by an anime fan-artist. Complete and polished like official game art — but clearly painted by a human hand, with visible brush strokes. NOT crayon, NOT coloured pencil, NOT a sketch, NOT 3D, NOT semi-realistic anime, NOT pixel art.

VIEW RULES (critical):
- PROPORTIONS: CHIBI, about 3.5–4.5 heads tall — big head, short body, short chunky legs, big chunky boots and gloves.
- NPC subjects: confident standing pose with personality (arms crossed, hand on hip, holding their role prop), body turned slightly 3/4, looking at the viewer.
- HERO subjects (class previews): action or ready stance (bow drawn across the body, sword raised, staff casting), same chibi proportions.
- The subject line says NPC or HERO — follow it.
- ALWAYS full body: head to boots in frame, centred.

STYLE RULES (critical):
- LINEWORK: hand-inked dark-brown / near-black outlines, medium weight, slightly thicker on the outer silhouette and thinner inside, with natural line-weight variation like a real pen stroke. Clean and finished, not sketchy.
- EYES: big anime eyes — dark upper lash line, coloured iris with a darker top and lighter bottom, ONE big white highlight plus one tiny one. Small simple nose, small mouth, soft pink blush. Friendly readable expression (smug smile, determined look, warm grin).
- HAIR: big spiky / layered anime hair in chunky locks, PAINTED with visible brush strokes and a few bright glossy streaks along the strands.
- COLOURING: fully painted, every area finished. Soft cel shading with brushy transitions (2–3 tones per material), visible painterly brush texture on hair, cloth and leather — like hand-painted MMO art, not smooth airbrush.
- MATERIALS: shiny leather boots, gloves and belts with a bright highlight streak; metal with a simple sharp shine; round or oval jewels in gold settings with a white shine dot.
- Cute and appealing, a little cocky or friendly, NEVER grim.

PALETTE RULES (the Verge canon — the SUBJECT line's named colours always win):
- The SUBJECT line is palette law. If the SUBJECT names a colour for a garment ("forest-emerald coat", "indigo tabard", "pale-turquoise skin"), paint it exactly that colour. The hex defaults below only apply to materials the SUBJECT does NOT name.
- Every NPC has a DISTINCT PRIMARY COLOUR — no two share one. Treat the "bronze ring-mail + brown leather" default as a FAILURE MODE: if the SUBJECT doesn't say bronze, don't paint bronze.
- Fallback hexes (use only for unmentioned materials):
  - Leather / wood: #8A6341 base / #6B4A2E shadow / #B88F6D highlight.
  - Linen / cloth / pale-stone trim: #E8D4B5 base / #BFA783 shadow / #F6E0B5 highlight.
  - Bronze / brass fittings: #B88F6D base / #8A5F2F shadow / #E8A868 highlight.
  - Foliage / ranger green: #4A6E3A base / #2A4E1A shadow / #78A050 highlight.
  - Cloth accents: crimson #D4324B or teal #3A8E6A, warm not muddy.
- Named-colour reference (use when a SUBJECT names one of these):
  - Forest emerald #2E6E4A, honey-gold #F6C04A, sage green #6A8A5A, Reliquary indigo #2E3A7A, deep ruby/burgundy #B8324B, midnight purple #3A2A5A, pale-silver ring-mail #BFC6CE, sky-blue #AEDAE8, crimson #D4324B, ash-white #ECE8DE, bone-gray #B5B0A0, soot-black #1A1A1A, pale-turquoise skin #A8E0D4, sea-foam pearl #D4EDE4, deep ocean green #1F5F55, wheat-gold robe #D4B04A, saffron orange #E8873A, ink navy #26305A, vermillion #D4323A, mustard yellow #D4A82A, chord-sand pale-blue #B4D4E8, deep umber skin #4A2E22, tarnished gilt #B8933A, dusty rose #D4A4A4, faded velvet violet #6A4A7A.
- ONE "hero accent" per character in a small spot — pale-cyan #5EC9FF by default (a crystal, gem or glowing trim with a soft painted glow). Alternatives: honey-gold #F6C04A (noble / Reliquary), warm orange #F6A04A (fire).
- NO desaturated neutrals or muddy mid-browns UNLESS the SUBJECT explicitly calls for them (Whisper-Vendor's ash-white + charcoal is one such deliberate exception).

BACKGROUND:
- TRUE PNG transparency OR plain flat white (it gets cut out afterwards). Do NOT draw a checkerboard/grid.
- The character stands alone. NO scenery, NO ground tile, NO drop shadow (the engine adds it), NO reflection, NO extra props besides what the character holds.
- Portrait 2:3 canvas (1024×1536), character centred, head near the top and boots near the bottom with a small margin.

AVOID: crayon, coloured pencil, marker, paper texture, sketch or unfinished colouring, plastic airbrushed skin, smooth gradient-only shading with no brush texture, sparkly eyes full of star highlights, glitter and bokeh, excessive tiny detail, perfectly symmetrical face and pose, glowing rim light everywhere, semi-realistic proportions, long legs, 3D render, photoreal, vector art, pixel art, grimdark, top-down / isometric view, text, letters, signature, watermark, logo, multiple characters in one image, extra fingers, bad hands.

SUBJECT: {{SUBJECT}}
```

## Notes
- Keep the **MASTER PROMPT identical** across the whole cast — only swap `{{SUBJECT}}` — so NPCs read as one cast and heroes as one roster.
- **Palette discipline:** if the AI tries to repaint a named colour (e.g. renders Govrik's emerald coat as brown), re-roll with *"ignore the master palette defaults; use ONLY the colours named in the SUBJECT line"* in a negative prompt pass.
- **SDXL/Forge:** an anime/illustration checkpoint (Illustrious / Pony family) plus a chibi LoRA works best. Add *"painterly, brush strokes"* to the positive prompt. The AVOID block doubles as your negative prompt.
- **Consistency:** render ~15 good ones, then train a LoRA on them for a locked, reusable style.
- **For in-UI cutouts:** generate on white → remove background (rembg).
- **Fastest fix for an existing result:** if the design is right but the finish is wrong (like the crayon Mira), reply in the same chat: *"Repaint this exact character as finished hand-painted digital chibi game art — clean inked lines, fully coloured, brushy cel shading, glossy hair streaks, shiny leather. No crayon, no pencil, no paper texture."* That keeps the design and changes only the finish.
- **If it looks too AI-slick:** reply *"less airbrushed — more visible brush strokes in the hair and cloth, simpler shading, fewer sparkles, slightly asymmetric pose."*
- **If it comes out tall:** reply *"make it chibi — about 4 heads tall, bigger head, shorter legs, chunkier boots."*

- **Replacing the existing `_UI` sprites:** the dialogue box fits the portrait by its longer side and pins the feet to the bottom (`obj_Dialogue/Draw_64`), so the new 2:3 images drop straight into the same `s<Name>_UI` sprites with no code change. Keep the canvas framing the same across the whole cast so every NPC appears at the same size.
- **Cross-reference:** detailed lore voice + placement coords per NPC in [`NPCS.md`](./NPCS.md); the chibi 1:1 portrait prompt family in [`ART_PROMPTS.md`](./ART_PROMPTS.md) is the companion doc for the dialogue-UI headshots (different art direction, same palette canon).
